Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?
New subscribers get a 3-month free trial of Melodify Premium Individual. You'll need to provide payment details to start your trial. After 3 months, your subscription will automatically continue at the standard monthly price unless you cancel.
How do I cancel my subscription?
You can cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings. If you cancel during your free trial, you won't be charged. After the trial, you'll continue to have access to Premium until the end of your billing period.
What payment methods are accepted?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal. In some regions, you can also pay through mobile payment systems and bank transfers.
